Biography

Voices for ancestral cultures and a dying earth

NO RAZA was formed in 1997 in Bello, Antioquia, Colombia, and is now based in Florida. Rooted in classic death metal, the band writes with social and political conscience, and its stage show evokes the ancestral essence of South America's indigenous cultures.

Their records have been mixed by Chris "Zeuss" Harris (When Chaos Reigns) and Fredrik Nordström at Fredman Studios (Transcending Material Sins). Their videos earned MTV3 awards in the US, Mexico and Colombia. NO RAZA is signed to Noble Demon, the German label founded by ex-Nuclear Blast Patrick Walch.

Latest album · 2025

TYRONA

The band about their new album

‘No Raza return with “Tyrona” – a crushing new death metal opus forged in reverence to the ancient spirits of South America. This album is a sonic tribute to the sacred heritage of the indigenous chieftains from the cold, mystical heights of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. With every riff and every roar, ‘Tyrona’ honours the brave guardians of the ancestors who lived and died for the protection of nature, spirit and tradition – the true defenders of the true gods: sun, moon, water, animals and earth.

This is more than just music. It is a war cry from the past – a transcendental journey through time, gold and spirit. A brutal tribute to those who have understood that legacy is not measured in wealth, but in the preservation of sacred knowledge. An album that awakens the ancient and challenges the material. ‘Tyrona’ is not just an album. It is a resurrection.’

Featuring: Imperial Holocaust · Chiefdom · Looters · Ring of Gold · Savage Strength
