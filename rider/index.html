What we bring

Our rack & input list

We run a Behringer X AIR18 that sends 11 channels to FOH. Guitars are on a Phoenyx PTM-10 IEM system and drums on a Behringer P2. We operate our own IEM mixes. Guitars and bass play through our own wireless instrument systems and Valeton processors. Sequences and click run into the X AIR over USB.

Bass note Our bassist doesn't use IEMs. He needs a traditional floor monitor system to play properly: a frontfill and a bass amp. A sidefill is optional.

Guitars

We use 2 Valeton GP-200 digital pedals for our sound. We don't need amplifiers, but we'll use them if you have them.

Bass

Valeton GP-5 into a SansAmp, which also works as our DI (XLR out). Ampeg SVT Pro with an 8×10 cabinet preferred. We don't depend on the head for our sound; whatever works will do the job.

Other

Backdrop, 5 × 3 meters. 3 wireless instrument systems (2 guitars, bass). Our own lights: 6 LED pars and 2 spider lights on two T-bar stands, one on each side of the stage.
